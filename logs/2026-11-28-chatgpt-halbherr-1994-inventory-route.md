# Halbherr 1994 inventory route audit — 2026-11-28

**Scope:** text-only reading of the official Accademia Roveretana degli Agiati contents page and the published 2010 article. No original letter, excavation diary, disc image or image derivative was accessed.

## Direct source checks

1. The official [1994 *Inventario delle carte di Federico Halbherr* contents page](https://agiati.org/inventario-carte-federico-halbherr) distinguishes:
   - **Section A:** papers of Federico Halbherr owned by the Accademia degli Agiati.
   - **Section A, Part I:** incoming correspondence, beginning on p. 27.
   - **Section A, I-A:** correspondence from persons, also beginning on p. 27.
   - **Section C:** a separate group of Halbherr papers held by Biblioteca Civica di Rovereto “G. Tartarotti” (pp. 147–152).

2. Elena Sorge's [2010 article](https://media.agiati.org/page/attachments/agiati-atti-a-2010-%28i%29-art-11-sorge.pdf), printed pp. 304–305, reproduces Pernier-to-Halbherr letters dated 4 and 6 July 1908. Footnote 60 on p. 304 identifies the originals as held in **Archivio AGIATI**. The 4 July letter says the disc had been found the previous evening; the 6 July letter reports that initial cleaning showed both faces inscribed.

3. The official contents page is not an item-level finding aid and does not list Pernier's letters in its text. The earlier official person index points “Pernier, Luigi” to item 858, but the corresponding Baruffaldi inventory describes item 858 as a separate 1905 bequest inventory prepared by curator Pernier. The separate Halbherr item 964 / box 192 summary does not name Pernier.

## Conclusion and next step

Because Sorge's footnote identifies the originals as Archivio AGIATI and the 1994 inventory's Section A I-A is the incoming-correspondence-from-persons section, **Section A, Part I-A (p. 27 onward) is the best first text-only catalogue route**. This is a search route, not a confirmed locator. Do not treat Section C, item 858 or item 964 as the 1908 letters without item-level evidence.

Request an item-level text-only lookup for correspondence from Luigi Pernier to Federico Halbherr dated 4 and 6 July 1908, including exact fonds/series/box/fascicolo/call number and access terms. No contact has been sent.
