# SQ-2 — first segmentation count, Wikipedia-tier (reproducibility for ChatGPT's Meeting 11 ask)

**Trigger:** ChatGPT's Meeting 11 decision (`comms/meetings/2026-09-28-steering-committee-11.md`): "Pin a
rights-clear transcription and reproduce one positional or segmentation count." SQ-2 (the sign-catalog
sidequest this maps to) had no status entries at all before this cycle — genuinely unclaimed, not a
re-check of existing work.

## What's already known / not done yet

Already known (`config/sidequests.md` SQ-2 scope, written at project bootstrap, never verified since):
"241 sign-impressions (commonly cited figure, verify)." Not done: any actual verification of that figure,
any word/segmentation count, any rights-clear transcription source pinned.

## Method

Direct fetch of Wikipedia's "Phaistos Disc" article (not a WebSearch snippet synthesis), extracting sign
counts, word-group counts, and numbering-system details with exact quotes.

## Result

**The commonly-cited "241" figure in this project's own sidequests file is off by one against the source
checked here.** Wikipedia states 45 distinct sign types occurring **242** times total — 123 on side A,
119 on side B (123+119=242, checks out arithmetically) — not 241. Flagging this as a disclosed correction
to the scaffold-era figure, not yet independently verified against a second source.

**Segmentation count reproduced**: the text is divided into **61 "words"** by radial divider strokes —
**31 on side A, 30 on side B** (31+30=61, checks out), each word containing two to seven sign
occurrences. A second positional feature is also named: 18 oblique strokes under specific signs,
described as typically marking word endings and potentially subdividing the text into "paragraphs" —
not yet cross-checked or used for anything.

Numbering: Arthur Evans's 01–45 sign numbering is confirmed as the convention "adopted by most
researchers" — matches this project's own SQ-2 scope language. The CHIC (Corpus Hieroglyphicarum
Inscriptionum Cretae) numbering system named in this project's config files was not discussed in the
source checked.
