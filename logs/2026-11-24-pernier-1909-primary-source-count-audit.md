# Pernier 1909 primary-source count audit — 2026-11-24

## Scope and permissions
Text-only audit of the published article. No image bytes were fetched, viewed, counted, analyzed, cropped or used for derivatives. Side A and both Side B image candidates remain under action-specific HOLD.

## Primary source
Luigi Pernier, “Il disco di Phaestos con caratteri pittografici,” *Ausonia* 3 (1909), 255–302. Public text-layer PDF: https://omnika.org/component/html5flippingbook/?filename=111-Il_Disco_Phaestus.pdf&id=111&task=convert.getpdf

## Directly read statements
- pp. 272–273 (PDF lines 557–564): Pernier says one peripheral sign on face A was completely removed by flaking and could not be recognized from its outline.
- p. 280 (PDF lines 831–842): Pernier counts 241 signs: 123 on face A and 118 on face B; he identifies 45 distinct types. The 241 total already includes the sign he says was lost.
- pp. 292–293 (PDF lines 1408–1417): Pernier reports 31 groups on face A and 30 on face B.
- Modern institutional comparator: Italian School of Archaeology at Athens / SAIA page reports 242 signs and 31/30 groups: https://www.saia.gr/ (project source page referenced in the current research notes).

## Interpretation and uncertainty
The primary-source count is 241. The modern SAIA page says 242. Because Pernier's 241 already includes the fully flaked sign, the one-sign difference is not explained simply by counting that missing impression. The cause remains unresolved; no independent recount is authorized. The 31/30 group counts agree across the two reports.

Eisenberg's 2008 Minerva article argues the disc is a hoax; Hnila's 2009 response presents a counterargument. These are competing published arguments, not an independently established authenticity verdict:
- Eisenberg 2008: https://sites.utexas.edu/scripts/files/2016/07/eisenberg_2008.pdf
- Hnila 2009: https://www.academia.edu/3499990/The_Phaistos_Disc_An_Enigma

## Decision
Update the public record to distinguish Pernier's primary 241 count from the modern 242 report. Keep all image access and recounting on HOLD. No decipherment or translation claim is made.
