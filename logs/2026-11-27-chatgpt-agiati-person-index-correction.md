# Archivio Agiati person-index correction — 2026-11-27

## Question
Does the online Accademia Roveretana degli Agiati inventory identify Pernier's 4 and 6 July 1908 letters to Halbherr, and is item 964 / box 192 a defensible locator?

## Direct source checks

1. The official [full archive inventory landing page](https://agiati.org/inventario-archivio-agiati) identifies the 1999 inventory and exposes separate sections for the Baruffaldi and Halbherr personal archives.
2. The official [person/place/institution index](https://media.agiati.org/page/attachments/indice-dei-nomi-di-persona-luogo-delle-istituzioni-inventario-dell-archivio.pdf) lists “Pernier, Luigi, 858” (printed index p. 786).
3. The official [Baruffaldi inventory excerpt](https://media.agiati.org/page/attachments/1-baruffaldi-luigi-antonio-archivi-personali-e-di-enti.pdf) describes item 858 as “Legato Baruffaldi 1905”: an inventory of objects bequeathed by Luigi Antonio Baruffaldi to the Academy, drawn up on 1 August 1905 by “curatore Pernier.” The person index maps that curator reference to Luigi Pernier. This is a 1905 archival trace, not the 1908 discovery correspondence.
4. The separate [Halbherr inventory excerpt](https://media.agiati.org/page/attachments/11-halbherr-federico-archivi-personali-e-di-enti.pdf) lists item 964, box 192, as incoming correspondence, persons Pe–Ph, printed date range “1905–1903,” with Francesco Pellati and Démètre Philippides as range endpoints, six folders and 235 leaves. Its summary does not name Pernier. The name index's Pernier entry points to 858, not 964. Item 964 therefore remains an unconfirmed and now downgraded possibility, not a Pernier file locator.
5. Elena Sorge's [2010 article](https://heyjoe.fbk.eu/index.php/ataga/article/download/5198/5199/5199) reproduces a Pernier postcard dated 4 July 1908 and a letter dated 6 July 1908; footnote 60 says the two documents are in Archivio AGIATI. The article is a published transcription, not direct inspection of the originals.
6. The Academy's [current contact page](https://agiati.org/contatti) lists Alessandro Andreolli for Biblioteca/Archivio storico, email alessandro.andreolli@agiati.org. No contact has been sent.

## Conclusion
The exact archival location of the 4 and 6 July 1908 originals is still unknown. The earlier item-964 hypothesis is not supported by the person index and must not be described as Pernier's file. Item 858 is a separate 1905 Baruffaldi bequest inventory prepared by curator Pernier. An archivist must identify the 1908 originals by sender, recipient and date and confirm text-only access conditions.
