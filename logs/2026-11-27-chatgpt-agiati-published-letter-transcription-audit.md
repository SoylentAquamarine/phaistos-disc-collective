# Published Pernier letters and Archivio AGIATI locator audit — 2026-11-27

**Scope:** text-only reading of a published scholarly transcription and public archival inventories. No original letter, diary, disc image or image derivative was accessed.

## Published primary-text transcription
Elena Sorge, “Gnorìzete ton Kyrion Friderikon?”, *Atti Accademia Roveretana degli Agiati* 260 (2010), ser. VIII, vol. X, A, fasc. I, printed pp. 304–305, reproduces two letters from Luigi Pernier to Federico Halbherr:
- **Voris, 4 July 1908:** Pernier says the disc had been found late the previous evening, anchoring the reported discovery to 3 July. The article's footnote 60 identifies the originals as held in Archivio AGIATI.
- **Voris (Phaestos), 6 July 1908:** Pernier reports that first cleaning showed both faces were inscribed and gives an approximate count of about 120 signs per face. The letter also mentions a fragmentary Linear A tablet found in the same area.

This is a published transcription within a scholarly article, not direct inspection of the originals. The transcription is evidence for the wording as published; any archival claim should be checked against the original letters.

Source PDF: https://media.agiati.org/page/attachments/agiati-atti-a-2010-%28i%29-art-11-sorge.pdf

## Candidate archival locator
The Accademia's online 1999 *Inventario dell'archivio* identifies the Halbherr fonds. Its entry **964**, in **box 192**, is “Corrispondenza in entrata: persone (Pe–Ph),” six fascicoli, 235 leaves. Pernier is a plausible alphabetical fit within Pe–Ph, but the public inventory excerpt does not name him individually. The printed date span appears as “1905–1903,” an anomaly requiring confirmation rather than silent correction.

The separate 1994 *Inventario delle carte di Federico Halbherr* identifies incoming correspondence as Section A, Part I, beginning on p. 27. The official archive inventory page and contact page are public:
- https://agiati.org/inventario-archivio-agiati
- https://agiati.org/contatti

The official contact page names Alessandro Andreolli for Biblioteca accademica / Archivio storico (alessandro.andreolli@agiati.org).

## Decision
Request confirmation from the Archivio AGIATI that item 964 / box 192 contains Pernier's 4 and 6 July 1908 letters, plus the exact fascicolo/call number and text-only access terms. Preserve the Phaistos Disc image authorization HOLD; no image access is implied by this archival lead.
