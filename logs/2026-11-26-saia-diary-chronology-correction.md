# SAIA diary chronology correction — text-only source audit

Date: 2026-11-26. This is a reading of an institutional secondary account, not direct access to Pernier's diary or letters.

## Source
Nicola Cucuzza, *Il disco di Festòs (1908)*, hosted by the Scuola Archeologica Italiana di Atene (SAIA): https://scuoladiatene.it/images/documents/Il_disco_di_Festo%CC%80s_1908.pdf . The relevant chronology appears on the PDF's printed pages 2–3 (web text lines 25–78 in the indexed copy).

## Chronology as reported by Cucuzza
- **3 July 1908:** after the disc was brought to Pernier, he added a note to a letter he had just completed to Luigi Pigorini, reporting the discovery and describing more than one hundred stamped signs on one face.
- **4 July:** the diary entry records the find and its context; a postcard/first report to Federico Halbherr says the second face may also bear signs.
- **5 July:** a further diary entry records that, after cleaning, signs were recognized on the second face.
- **6 July:** a fuller letter to Halbherr included a *calco a graffite* (rubbing) and reported a Linear A-inscribed tablet fragment found in the same room.

This corrects shorthand that described only a 5 July diary entry. It does not promote any of these archival documents to direct source reading: Cucuzza is summarizing them.

## Catalogue and collateral correspondence
SAIA's archive page says the archives are generally public and freely consultable, subject to a stated exception, and its currently exposed open-access catalogue lists the Federico Halbherr fonds and notebooks: https://scuoladiatene.it/biblioteca-e-archivi/archivi.html . The public index examined does not expose a Pernier diary inventory. This is a limit of the visible index, not proof SAIA lacks the diary.

SAIA's 2014–2016 *Notiziario* describes nine autograph letters **from Gian Giacomo Porro to Luigi Pernier**, sent from Rhodes between 6 February and 6 May 1913 (pp. 35–36): https://www.scuoladiatene.it/images/documents/notiziari/a13-14n1.pdf . These are collateral Pernier-related holdings, not the July 1908 diary or discovery correspondence, and must not be used as substitutes.
