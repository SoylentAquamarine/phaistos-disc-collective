# Archivio AGIATI item 964 scope audit — 2026-11-27

**Scope:** direct text reading of the official public Halbherr-fonds inventory PDF and the official contact page, cross-checked against the published Sorge transcription. No original letter, diary, disc image or image derivative was accessed.

## Official inventory evidence
The Accademia Roveretana degli Agiati's official Halbherr-fonds inventory PDF identifies:
- Box 192, item 964.
- “Corrispondenza in entrata: persone (Pe–Ph)” (incoming correspondence, persons Pe–Ph).
- Named alphabetical range examples: Francesco Pellati and Démètre Philippides.
- Six fascicoli, 235 leaves.
- Printed date span: “1905–1903,” which is anomalous and must not be silently corrected.

Pernier is alphabetically plausible within Pe–Ph, but is not individually named in this public entry. Item 964 is therefore a candidate locator only. It does not establish that the 4 and 6 July 1908 letters are present or identify an exact fascicolo.

## Published transcription, not original inspection
Elena Sorge, “Gnorìzete ton Kyrion Friderikon?” (2010), reproduces Pernier-to-Halbherr letters dated 4 and 6 July 1908. FBK's HeyJoe portal makes the article available under CC BY-NC-ND 4.0:
https://heyjoe.fbk.eu/index.php/ataga/article/download/5198/5199/5199

This supports a reading of the published transcription only. It is not direct inspection of the original letters or Pernier's excavation diary. Do not alter or redistribute an adapted version of the article.
