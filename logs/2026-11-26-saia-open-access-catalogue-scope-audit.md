# SAIA open-access catalogue scope audit — 2026-11-26

## Question
Does the Scuola Archeologica Italiana di Atene (SAIA) provide a directly usable public inventory reference for Luigi Pernier's July 1908 excavation diary and correspondence concerning the Phaistos Disc?

## Sources read
1. Nicola Cucuzza, *Il disco di Festòs (1908)*, institutional SAIA-hosted PDF: https://scuoladiatene.it/images/documents/Il_disco_di_Festo%CC%80s_1908.pdf
2. SAIA, "Archivi": https://scuoladiatene.it/biblioteca-e-archivi/archivi.html
3. SAIA, online index for Fondo Federico Halbherr: https://scuoladiatene.it/images/documents/taccuini-hallber/HALBHERR%20IC%20INDICI.pdf
4. SAIA, "Archivio fotografico e documentazione" request form: https://scuoladiatene.it/archivio-fotografico-e-documentazione.html
5. SAIA, *Notiziario* XIII–XIV (2014–2016): https://www.scuoladiatene.it/images/documents/notiziari/a13-14n1.pdf

## Directly observed in the institutional secondary account
Cucuzza's article describes a July 3, 1908 letter to Luigi Pigorini, a July 4 first report to Federico Halbherr, a July 5 entry in Pernier's diary, and a fuller July 6 letter to Halbherr that included a rubbing of the disc and news of a Linear A-inscribed tablet fragment found in the same room. This is a secondary account of archival documents, not a direct reading of the diary or letters; the PDF does not provide a usable SAIA inventory/call number in the passages examined.

## Catalogue scope
SAIA's archive page says its archives are public and freely consultable, subject to a stated Dodecanese exception, and describes ongoing digitization. Its currently exposed open-access list is the Fondo Federico Halbherr, including 72 notebooks from 1884–1936. The index lists Festòs entries in some Halbherr notebooks, but these are not Pernier's diary and must not be substituted for it. The open-access index does not expose a Pernier fonds or a July 1908 diary inventory entry. This is a limit of the public index, not proof that SAIA lacks the documents.

The 2014–2016 SAIA *Notiziario* also describes archival correspondence addressed to Pernier held by SAIA. This supports the existence of Pernier-related holdings, but it does not identify the requested diary or its inventory number.

## Request path and blocker
The official SAIA document-request form requires requester name, email, institutional affiliation, reason, research topic, requested document, inventory number, personal-data consent and CAPTCHA. No request was submitted. The exact inventory number and an eligible institutional requester/contact are still missing.

## Decision
The shortest defensible next step is a text-only catalogue inquiry for:
- Pernier's excavation diary entry dated 5 July 1908;
- the 3 July 1908 letter to Luigi Pigorini;
- the 4 July telegram/first report and 6 July letter to Federico Halbherr;
- the associated "calco a graffite" attachment.

Do not request or access disc images as part of this inquiry.
