Wins so far · in plain English

What we have actually accomplished

We have documented the object's limits and attached specific scholarly criticisms to several proposed decipherments. No reading has passed the evidence bar.

New text-only archival lead: Aegeus indexes two 2009 papers by V. La Rosa—one on the disc's authenticity, which cites Pernier's excavation daybooks, and one publishing selected notebook passages. SAIA confirms its archives hold excavation diaries, but the exact Pernier 1908 inventory remains unidentified. These are bibliographic/secondary-source leads, not a direct diary reading.

New published transcription: Elena Sorge's 2010 article reproduces Pernier-to-Halbherr letters dated 4 and 6 July 1908; its footnote identifies the originals as held in Archivio AGIATI. The 4 July letter says the disc was found the previous evening (3 July); the 6 July letter reports both faces after cleaning. The official Accademia name index locates Luigi Pernier at item 858. The corresponding Baruffaldi inventory describes item 858 as a 1905 list of objects bequeathed to the Academy, drawn up on 1 August 1905 by “curatore Pernier”; it is not the 1908 letters. The separate Halbherr entry 964 / box 192 is Pe–Ph incoming correspondence with an anomalous 1905–1903 date span and does not name Pernier in its summary. Downgrade item 964 to an unconfirmed lead, not Pernier’s file. The exact location of the 4 and 6 July 1908 originals remains unconfirmed; an archivist must identify them.

New archival scope check: the official Halbherr-fonds inventory lists item 964 (box 192) as incoming Pe–Ph correspondence, with Francesco Pellati and Démètre Philippides as range examples, six fascicoli and 235 leaves; its printed date span is anomalous (“1905–1903”). Pernier is an alphabetical fit but is not individually named, so item 964 remains only a candidate locator pending archivist confirmation. Read the official inventory ↗. No archive contact has been sent. The disc-image authorization HOLD remains binding.

New archival route: the official 1994 Inventario delle carte di Federico Halbherr separates Section A (Halbherr papers owned by the Accademia) from Section C (papers held by Biblioteca Civica G. Tartarotti). Section A, Part I-A (incoming correspondence from persons) begins on p. 27. Sorge's 2010 footnote identifies the Pernier letters' originals as held in Archivio AGIATI. This narrows the next text-only catalogue lookup; it does not identify an item or prove the letters appear in that subsection. Read the official 1994 inventory contents ↗.

Why this is a harder starting line than any sibling project

There is no second text. No second text is known.

Our sibling investigations of the Voynich manuscript and rongorongo each work from a corpus of many pages or many objects. The Phaistos Disc is one object. Standard decipherment methods lean on repetition and structure across many texts to tell a correct reading from a merely plausible one — with this single object, that check does not exist and cannot be built. This project treats that constraint as the central fact shaping everything else, not a footnote.
